01

Audience selection / Representation learning

Finding higher-value audiences

A two-tower system for WhatsApp retargeting, learning from 350M+ user-brand interactions to select audiences beyond a learning-to-rank baseline.

Model & measurement

Trained with in-batch negatives. Improved top-quartile ROAS lift by 13% over an XGBoost learning-to-rank baseline.

Selected audiences averaged 2.5× last-click ROAS, 30% above brands’ current targeting. These are separate reported measures, not causal incrementality; last-click attribution does not establish incremental lift.

03

Checkout risk / Explainable ML

Making checkout risk actionable

Gradient-boosted return-to-origin (RTO) prediction, paired with explanations that make risk signals usable in real-time and batch checkout decisions.

Evaluation & production

Improved RTO precision by 21% at 10% recall versus the previous production model. The core risk system serves 10,000+ brands.

SHAP-based XGBoost explanations handle 500K+ daily requests, with <20ms p95 latency on the real-time path.

Product intelligence at catalog scale

Fine-tuned Qwen3 30B MoE with vLLM for attribute extraction across 3M+ products: from 2 minutes to 0.3 seconds per item, with a 60% cost reduction. LLM-extracted attributes and content-based product representations support merchant similarity, customer attribute profiling, and cold-start personalization for 500+ brands.

Shared features, reliable foundations

PySpark and Dataproc pipelines across 500M+ transactions and 100M+ customers produce discount-affinity, spending-power, and risk-propensity signals for CLV, retargeting, and checkout.

Monitoring beyond deployment

GCP Cloud Scheduler, BigQuery, and Cloud Run monitor drift, fill rates, and prediction–outcome alignment across 10,000+ brands.

Different segments, different treatments

Segment-tailored WhatsApp abandoned-cart and checkout treatments improved conversion by 3 percentage points and prepaid share by 7 percentage points.
