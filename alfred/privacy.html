Privacy policy

Alfred Personal Workspace · Effective 23 October 2026

This policy describes the personal Google Workspace integration operated by Aayush Jain. It applies to the assistant’s handling of Google account data, not to Google’s own services or unrelated portfolio content.

Data accessed

With the owner’s OAuth consent, the integration can access spreadsheet contents and metadata through Google Sheets, and file contents and metadata through Google Drive. The current authorization requests the Google Sheets and Google Drive scopes. These are broad permissions, not access restricted by Google to a single file. OAuth access and refresh credentials are stored on the assistant server to support authorized access without repeated sign-in.

Purpose and changes

Data is used to answer the owner’s questions, organize records, prepare summaries and reports, and carry out approved file or spreadsheet changes. Granting OAuth access is not, by itself, approval for an arbitrary edit, deletion, publication, or sharing action.

Processing and disclosure

Relevant Google account data may be included in requests to external AI providers configured for the assistant, including OpenAI, when needed to perform the owner’s requested tasks. Responses and relevant excerpts may also pass through configured communication services, such as Slack or email. Those services process information under their own terms and policies. This is not an exclusively on-device system.

The operator does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Transfers are limited to providing the requested user-facing functionality, the owner’s explicit directions, applicable legal obligations, or security needs as permitted by Google’s policies.

Alfred Personal Workspace’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage, retention, and safeguards

Retrieved data, derived records, reports, conversation history, logs, and backups may persist on the privately managed server and in the communication or processing services involved in a task. There is no blanket automatic deletion period for all of these records. Records are retained for the owner’s ongoing use or until removed; backups and external-service retention may outlast deletion of an active local copy.

Server credentials are kept outside the public website and repository, with operating-system access restrictions. No private spreadsheet contents or OAuth credentials are intentionally published on this site. These safeguards do not constitute a guarantee against every security incident.

Revoking access and deleting records

The owner can revoke the Google connection at Google Account connections. Revocation prevents subsequent authorized access but does not automatically erase data already retrieved. The owner can separately request removal of stored credentials and retained records through the assistant or the contact below; copies in backups or third-party services require separate handling under those services’ retention policies.

Website hosting

These informational pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no sign-in form, analytics scripts, advertising, or embedded third-party resources. GitHub may process ordinary website request information under its privacy statement.

Contact and policy changes

Contact the operator, Aayush Jain, at aayushjaincse@gmail.com for questions or deletion requests. Material changes to the integration’s data practices should be reflected in this policy and its effective date.
