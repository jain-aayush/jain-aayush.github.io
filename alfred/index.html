Alfred Personal Workspace

Alfred Personal Workspace is Aayush Jain’s private assistant integration for working with his own Google Sheets and Google Drive files. It runs through Hermes Agent on a privately managed server.

What it does

With the account owner’s authorization, Alfred reads spreadsheet data and Drive documents to organize information, prepare summaries, and support personal reviews. It can also make spreadsheet or file changes when the owner explicitly approves the requested operation.

Google account access

The integration requests Google Sheets and Drive permissions through Google’s OAuth consent flow. These permissions can technically permit broader file access than a single spreadsheet; their use is limited operationally to the owner’s requested tasks. This website does not collect Google passwords, provide an OAuth login form, or display private account data.

Relevant Google account data may be processed by external AI providers and delivered through the owner’s configured communication services to perform requested tasks. Read the privacy policy for processing, retention, and revocation details.
